## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-12

### Added

- The banner, the preferences, the browser script and the records of atriametal/sylius-cookie-consent-plugin 0.1.1,
  for any Symfony application.
- `records.class` option, to store the records in an entity of the application.
- `channel_code_provider` option, to record the site or channel on which the visitor consented.
- Translations in English, French and German, worded for any site rather than for a shop.
